CRT shaders in this folder come from libretro/glsl-shaders
https://github.com/libretro/glsl-shaders

Each .glsl file keeps its original header, including its author and
licence. The repository as a whole is distributed under GPL-2.0; some
individual shaders carry their own (often more permissive) terms, stated
in the file header.

Only the files needed by the presets in the tier folders are included.
Presets were rewritten so their shader paths point here; nothing else
about them was changed.
